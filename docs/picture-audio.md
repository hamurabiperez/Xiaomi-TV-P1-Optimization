# Picture and audio — subjective tuning, not instrument calibration

A final pass adjusted the TV for viewing in a bedroom with curtains typically closed and lights sometimes on/off, using **internal stereo speakers**. No colorimeter, light meter or audio measurement microphone was used.

## SDR apps and HOME

| Setting | Final |
|---|---|
| Picture mode | **Movie** (`Película`) |
| Backlight | 80 (from the Movie preset) |
| Brightness / contrast / saturation | 50 / 50 / 50 |
| Color temperature | Warm |
| Color gamut | Auto |
| Gamma | Medium |
| Motion smoothing (visible setting) | Off |
| Noise reduction (visible setting) | Off |
| Adaptive ambient light | On |

The owner reported the picture comfortable and natural both in darkness and with room lighting. These settings are **not universal recommendations** and are not verified against a reference display.

## Built-in audio

Movie (`Película`) mode was preferred after comparison with Standard and News. Output remained **TV speakers**, delay **0**, EQ 7 bands untouched at **0**. Dialogue and ambience sounded balanced to the owner. No proven night-mode loudness compression or Dolby control was available in the inspected official menus; hidden system keys were **not** altered.

## Xbox Series S, HDMI3

**Game** mode kept the preset values. The TV's game-mode flag was enabled while the ALLM state already observed was retained. SDR calibration patterns showed complete edges and acceptable brightness according to the owner. **No measured latency and no real-game validation** were performed.

## HDR: restore original, don't overfit

A trial HDR Movie profile was rejected after perceived color shifts/jerkiness. Original **Standard HDR** was restored, and **77 image-related keys** matched the pre-trial reference. Do not copy SDR settings to HDR. A YouTube sample was observed as **1920×1080@60, AV1 10-bit, PQ / BT.2020**; the 4K panel does **not** imply that stream was received at 2160p. Controlled 2160p testing and HDMI3 HDR remain outstanding.

Settings in this section document one user's tested preference, not calibrated color accuracy, absolute luminance, acoustic frequency response or HDMI latency.
